sudoKooL

Fill each row, column, and 3×3 box with the numbers 1–9.

Select an empty cell to begin.

Core 9 Rules

Play by the regular Sudoku rules, plus one extra challenge:

  • The center cell of each 3×3 box makes nine center cells total.
  • All nine center cells must contain different numbers (1–9).
  • Conflicting numbers stay on the board and are marked red. You can fix them whenever you like.
